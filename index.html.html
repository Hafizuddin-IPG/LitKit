Pedagogical Showcase & Kit Overview

LitKit: Modular English Literacy Learning Innovation

Designed for Year 3 Pupils with Low English Proficiency

Primary Target Skill

Writing & Decoding

Core Problem

Year 3 low-proficiency pupils have restricted vocabulary, struggle with basic grammatical word categories (nouns/verbs/adjectives), and experience difficulty sounding out unfamiliar words to compose simple sentences.

Main Innovation

A modular learning kit organizing physical hands-on components and web-based audio-visual activities into a scaffolded, progressive learning pathway with self-correction feedback.

Physical-Digital Integration

Physical cards and sentence strips are seamlessly paired with QR code web shortcuts, TTS audio models, and digital self-checking writing tools to prevent cognitive overload.

Kit Modules Architecture & Pedagogical Flow

1. Word Explorer (Vocabulary)

Foundation Stage

Focus: Overcoming limited vocabulary and word category confusion.

  • Colour-coded cards: Nouns (Blue), Verbs (Green), Adjectives (Yellow).
  • Picture-to-word matching tasks with tactile flashcards.
  • Personal Word Bank generator for self-directed vocabulary building.

2. Reading Rescue (Phonics & Audio)

Decoding Stage

Focus: Decoding unfamiliar words and correct pronunciation support.

  • Syllable breakdown cards (e.g. rab - bit).
  • Web Speech API synthesized voice playback with speed control (slow/normal).
  • Guided short reading strips bridging single words to connected text.

3. Sentence Builder (Writing)

Application Stage

Focus: Sentence construction and basic writing conventions.

  • Subject + Verb + Object physical and digital sentence arrangement.
  • 3-point writing convention checklist (Capitalisation, Spacing, Punctuation).
  • Scaffolded transition from card arrangement to independent sentence writing.

4. Digital Corner & Arcade

Reinforcement Stage

Focus: Repeated practice, motivation, and multi-modal reinforcement.

  • Integrated interactive Wordwall-style arcade games.
  • Digital grammar presentation slides for whole-class teaching.
  • Tested QR code linkages connecting physical kit cards to web apps.

Three Levels of Differentiated Support

Level 1: Novice
Picture Matching

For pupils requiring high visual support. Focuses on matching picture prompts to printed words with single-syllable audio models.

Level 2: Developing
Guided Word Arrangement

For pupils learning sentence structure. Uses colour-coded Subject-Verb-Object blocks to build complete thoughts.

Level 3: Advanced
Independent Sentence Writing

For pupils ready for challenge. Prompts independent writing with self-correction against capital letters, spacing, and full stops.
